It is not ready, and it may not pay you for a long time.

  • No block has ever been found by the node pool.
  • No payout has ever been made.
  • Your balance may stay unpaid for a long time.
  • An unaudited node can cheat you. The tool to audit one, ghost-audit, is not built yet.
  • If you need steady income, use an established pool.

Nothing has run on signet or mainnet yet. Before anything reaches mainnet it must pass multi-node tests on regtest, a hostile-node test, several nodes on our own signet with real block wins, and real mining hardware on signet.

Even if every miner could carry large coinbases, a 100 TH/s miner on a pool at 1% of the network would wait about 4.5 months for a first payment. The pool is about 100,000 times short of 1% of the network. The full reading →

What it is

Run a node, or just point a miner.

Anyone runs a node

No approval, no roster, no signed list. A node runs a Bitcoin node and a Stratum pool, picks its own transactions, and takes the transaction fees of every block its own miners find. Run a node →

A share chain keeps the books

Nodes pass each other the larger shares. Each share proves its own work and its own payee. About every 54 seconds a harder share becomes an anchor that orders the recent ones, and the chain with the most work wins. No votes, no signatures to trust, no elders.

Paid from the coinbase

Every block the pool finds pays owed ledger balances directly in its coinbase, exactly what is owed and never more. There is no pool wallet and no withdrawal. Point a miner →

* Plus any part of the miners' 99.5% that a block cannot pay out, for example when few miners are owed much. That change can be large: on a pool of mostly stock Antminers, simulation sends 20-33% of the miners' reward to the treasury. The treasury address is a fixed protocol constant and has not been published yet.

At today's subsidy of 312,500,000 sats, a block sends 1,562,500 to the treasury and 310,937,500 to miners. At current fees miners keep about 98.95% of total block value.

How it works

Start to finish.

  1. 1
    Connect
    A miner connects to any listed node, or its own. Its username is its payout address: P2SH, P2WPKH, P2WSH or P2TR only.
  2. 2
    Get a job
    The node builds a coinbase every other node can recompute: treasury, the owed miners in a fixed order, the finder, the commitments. The node picks the transactions; it cannot pick the miner payouts.
  3. 3
    Mine
    The miner hashes as it would on any pool. Its firmware sees ordinary coinbase bytes.
  4. 4
    Share
    A share above the network tier goes to every node. Any node checks it alone: the work, the coinbase, the tag and the payouts. A share with wrong payouts earns nothing.
  5. 5
    Anchor
    About every 54 seconds a share 256 times harder becomes an anchor and locks in a batch of recent shares. If two compete, the one with the most work wins.
  6. 6
    Credit
    Each share credits sats at once, from its own difficulty. Every node reaches the same balance by running the same arithmetic on the same chain.
  7. 7
    Block found
    The block pays from the balance table as it stood 20 blocks earlier, so a share-chain reorg can't change what it should pay.
  8. 8
    Settle
    Nodes see the block and retire exactly what it paid. Coinbase outputs spend after 100 confirmations, as usual.
  9. 9
    Verify
    ghost-audit will let a miner check that their node really credited them. It is not built yet; until it is, a miner trusts the node it mines on.
The payout rule

Exact pay. Nobody overpaid.

Credited at share time
Your balance grows as each share is accepted, by an amount worked out from the share's own difficulty.
Snapshot at H − 20
A block at height H pays from the balances as they stood at H − 20, less what blocks up to H − 1 already paid.
Slot classes
A block pays 3, 9, 44, 143, 364 or 500 balances, depending on what the finder's firmware can safely carry.
Oldest half, largest half
Half the slots go to the balances waiting longest, half to the largest. The single largest is paid last and takes any partial payment.
1,000 sat minimum
Balances under 1,000 sats wait. A balance only ever falls by exactly what was paid.
Change to the treasury
Whatever a block's slots can't use goes to the treasury with its 0.5%. Fees go to the finder node.

Status

The road to launch.

done
Specification
The share chain, the payout rule and the firmware limits, with simulations. Old node rewards, elders, capabilities, votes, the L2, Ghost Pay and Wraith are removed.
now
Build
The node pool is being built. Share-chain sync, ghost-audit and open node listing are not finished.
next
Prove it
Regtest, then our own signet, with real hardware. Nothing reaches mainnet until every stage passes.
then
Handover
The old 8-node network keeps running until an agreed height. Its balances are exported, published and imported as the node pool's starting ledger. The height has not been set.
last
Public launch
The NOT READY banner comes off only after the proving and handover stages pass.
