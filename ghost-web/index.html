solo bitcoin mining

Mine solo. If you find a block, it pays you.

Ghost Pool is a solo mining pool. Your username is your Bitcoin address: no account, no sign-up, no KYC. When your miner finds a block, the block itself pays you the subsidy and every fee, less 0.5% of the subsidy for the pool. Until then you are paid nothing. Solo mining is a lottery, and most small miners never win it.

the payout rule

The whole block, less 0.5% of the subsidy.

Every job the pool sends your miner already has your address in its coinbase. If that job becomes a block, the coinbase pays two outputs and nothing else:

plainly

What to expect.

  • It is a lottery. At today's difficulty a 1 TH/s Bitaxe would wait, on average, thousands of years for a block, and a single 200 TH/s Antminer decades. Your miner page shows your own odds.
  • No steady income. If you need regular payouts, use a pooled (PPS or PPLNS) pool. Here you are paid only when you find a block.
  • Templates. The nodes build blocks from the highest-fee standard transactions in their mempool. Job Declaration (SV2), where your miner builds its own template, is coming soon.
