security

Reporting a problem.

A bug that cannot be used to hurt anyone

Open an issue on GitHub, with what you did, what you expected and what happened.

Something exploitable

Do not open a public issue. Report it privately through a GitHub security advisory. Anything that could redirect a block's reward, change the coinbase split, withhold or delay a found block, crash a node, or reach a node's private interfaces counts.

What happens next

You get an answer, the issue is fixed on the nodes, and you are credited in the fix unless you ask not to be. Please give the fix time to reach the nodes before you publish.
